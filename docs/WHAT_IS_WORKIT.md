# What is Work-It? (for new athletes)

New-athlete explanation of the program. Not the short Who-sheet copy (`lib/helpCopy.ts`).

**Keep current:** when week shape, lock rule, Your pick, belts, gym/travel, or Home Start change, update this file to match `app/help/page.tsx`, `lib/bonusDay.ts`, `lib/yourPick.ts`, and `CLAUDE.md`.

---

Work-It is a strength log for your house. You sign in with **email + a 4-digit PIN** and write down what you lifted. That is the whole job: show up, do the day’s work, record it. New people join at `/join?h=gowanus`. Returning people use `/login`. Work-It is for adults: before signing up, every new athlete confirms they are **18 or older** and that the workouts and weights are suggestions they follow **at their own pace and their own risk**. The waiver they sign also carries a short **Code of Conduct** (respect, no harassment, log only real training, don't share other people's photos or information, one account each); breaking it can get an account blocked.

Do not invent the session on the fly. We already thought it through and planned it for you. The year is written: **48 weeks**. You pick how many days a week you train (**1 to 5**, 4 is the default) at sign-up, and can change it anytime in Edit profile — Home asks again every 6 weeks in case your life changed. Finish that many sessions and the week **locks**. Gaps count — you do not have to be perfect, you have to hit your number. A week you have already locked stays locked, belt and all, even if you change your pace later.

## What the week looks like

**Weeks 1–6** are the saddle — two upper days, two lower days:

- **Mon** Upper A (chest, back, shoulders, arms)
- **Tue** Lower A (legs, glutes, posterior chain)
- **Wed** rest
- **Thu** Upper B (same idea, different mix)
- **Fri** Lower B

If you're at 1 to 3 days a week, you'll see **full-body days** instead of the split — nothing gets skipped on a short week.

**Training focus.** Not training in a gym? Pick one or more focuses from the menu (or the setup screen everyone sees once): **Build muscle · Gym** is the program above; **Core Inspired** rotates Pilates, yoga and core on a mat; **Home · 2 Dumbbells** is full-body days with two dumbbells, alternating two workouts (A/B/A at 3 days, A/B/A/B/A at 5, then B first the next week); **No equipment · Travel** is bodyweight full-body days, plus Pilates and core on a 4th or 5th day. Full-body days on back-to-back days work the same muscles with no break, so the setup warns you about the risk of overuse and injury — leave a rest day between them when you can. Pick more than one and your week alternates between them (and each week a different one leads). You also pick the weekdays you train — that count is your weekly number — and a 9am reminder goes out on those days if notifications are on. On Home, a full-width pill under the Effective number lets you choose different focuses for just the next week that has not started. Every focus locks weeks and counts toward belts exactly like the program: train one day a week and one workout locks it, train two and it takes two. A locked week stays locked if you change focus. **Your pick** also offers Pilates, Home and Travel workouts (filter by category), in any week a pick is allowed.

**Week 7 onward:** Upper A, **one** lower (A and B take turns), Upper B, and a **Your pick** day on Friday (it replaced the old Extra Upper — pick Upper if you want that workout). From week 7, Upper B opens with a **decline** dumbbell press for the lower chest instead of the incline; the incline is one **Alt** tap away. Lower B swaps the leg extension for the **leg press**, a heavier quad lift; leg extension and goblet step-ups are on its Alt list.

**Add or remove exercises (week 7 onward):** on a live workout you can **Remove exercise** from any card with no finished sets (never the last card), or **Add exercise** from any lift in The Library (up to 4 per workout). It's at your own risk: the first change in a workout asks you to confirm. Changes are for that workout only.

**Abs and core:** from week 4, every workout carries one ab/core move from a pool of 12 (planks, crunches, reverse crunches, bicycle crunches, Russian twists, dead bugs, Pallof press, woodchops, hanging knee and leg raises, ab wheel). It changes every week, no two workouts in a week get the same one, and it switches between the start and the end of the workout. Moves that need a bar, wheel or cable have a no-equipment version on travel days.

**The count is what matters.** Your week locks once you finish your days-per-week number of workouts — any mix of plan days, swaps and Your picks.

**Your pick**, any week: besides your program, start an **Upper, Lower, Yoga, Core, Full body or Run** workout (a run is 10, 20 or 30 minutes on a countdown; it earns 50 lb for every minute you actually run, at least 500 / 1,000 / 1,500 lb) whenever you want — pick the exact one from a dropdown. A **Focus** row (Gym, Core, Dumbbell, Travel — it starts on yours, tap more to widen) and a **Category** row narrow the list (Upper A or B, Lower A or B, or one of the extra packs like Hinge or Glutes). One switch **adds** it on top of your week, or **swaps** it in for a program day you haven't started (that day then counts as done). Every finished Your pick counts toward locking the week, belts and medals — any 4 sessions still lock a week (or your own day count). It files on the week you're in — through Sunday that stays the week you just locked, until you start the next one — or an earlier week that isn't locked yet, never a future one. Yoga and Core run as timed holds you tap through, or as **mark done** (30 minutes minimum, once a day); both score like a full workout — your average session from the last 7 days, scaled by how hard it felt. If you chose 5 days a week, you get two Your pick days from week 7 (one in weeks 3–6). The old bonus day is gone; past bonus sessions still count.

**Home** shows one **Workout** box: **Start Next** (the next day in your plan, with its focus and estimated time) and **Pick one** (opens Your pick — Upper, Lower, Yoga, Core, Full body or Run). On a Your pick day, Pick one is the only button. Under it, one number — **Effective**, the last 15 days vs last time those lifts ran — and an **Invite a friend** row. On a Monday, if you trained two weeks in a row and missed last week, your coach adds a short note: one week off is fine, back to the iron. A week locked early shows Pick one for an extra session; finishing all 48 weeks shows Browse.

**Streak badges** (2 to 6 weeks in a row) count your best back-to-back run of locked weeks, so a missed week never stops a later run from earning them. A badge you've earned is never taken back.

**Joining mid-week? Take a Test Drive.** Week 1 always starts on a Monday. If your first workout isn't on a Monday, you get a **Test Drive** so you can start right away: 3 workouts if you start on a Tuesday (Full Body A, Upper A, Full Body B), 2 on Wednesday–Friday (Full Body A and B), 1 on the weekend (Full Body A). Home counts down to Monday. Test Drive workouts count for you (your totals, stats, badges and lifting history) but not for Week 1, week locks, belts, or the house board and medals. Leftover Test Drive workouts disappear on Monday, and an unfinished one is deleted. No nudge mail goes out before that Monday.

## How you actually train

Gold means **do this**: Start, log the set, Finish it.

Each movement has sets. You type **weight and reps** (or time / distance when that is the move), then tap Complete Set. **How hard** is optional; skip it and the set counts as Fair. A set the app marks **Skipped** doesn't count toward your totals, records or history; if you did the work, open it with Editing and tap Complete Set and it counts again. The app remembers last time and copies load forward so you are not guessing. The idea is **progressive overload**: start conservative (weeks 1–2), then add a little weight or a couple of reps (weeks 3–5), then try to match or beat an earlier week. After that, same big lifts; the notes change by block.

Every lift card shows **Last time** — your best set from the last session of that lift, plus how hard you rated it (`Last time: 105 lb × 15 · Effort 5`). During **Overload Progressions** that line moves into a gold **Aim for** box: hit the top of the rep range on every set at Hard or easier and it says add weight — 2.5 lb on dumbbell curls and raises, 5 lb on other upper-body lifts, 10 lb on legs. If last time was Max, it says match it — never more after a Max (or go lighter if you fell short of the range). Otherwise same weight, one more rep. It also says how hard the sets should feel (Hard = about 2 reps left; Overload changes it by week), and flips to **You're past it** once a set today beats the target. It is a suggestion; the app's prefill does not change.

If you cannot get to a gym, flip the day to **Travel** — same session, no-equipment swaps.

**Bodyweight moves count your weight.** Save your weight in Edit profile and moves like push-ups, dips, bodyweight squats and lunges count part of it on every rep (a push-up counts about 64%). Anything you add on top — a vest, a backpack — goes in the weight field as extra. Only you and Kevin see your weight, and Your performance shows how it has changed over time. No weight saved yet? A banner on Home and on your next few workouts links straight to the field. Every six weeks the check-in asks for your current weight too; saving it just says “Weight saved.”

You can add an optional **warmup or cooldown** (easy run/bike, yoga, abs, or a short stretch/core circuit). Stretch and Core ask Easy, Medium, or Hard before they start; the easy run asks 10, 20, or 30 minutes and starts a timer for that long. Run and bike earn 50 lb for every minute you actually go (500 per 10), so going past your pick still counts. Nice extra, not required to lock the week. Starting a day that is already open resumes the session that has your sets.

## What you are chasing

**The house** ranks everyone who hit their own days-per-week for the stretch first (30 days = 4 weeks of it; all time = one locked week), then by average weight per session. Weekly medals use the same rule. Piling on extra sessions never buys a spot. Under the time pills, weekday boxes show your finished workouts for Monday–Sunday and the house average, rounded up. Last 7 days is this calendar week.

**Overload Progressions** (opens once you have 6 locked weeks, same as Hyrox — a card on Home for 3 days and an item under **More programs** in the menu): a 6-week muscle-building series that starts on a Monday and replaces the program while it runs — one opt-in track at a time, so not alongside Hyrox. Your days per week set the split (1–3 full body, 4 upper/lower, 5 upper/lower/push/pull/legs). Heavy lifts rest 3 minutes, secondary 2, isolation 75 seconds. Weeks 1–2 aim Fair, 3–4 Hard, 5–6 push the last isolation set further. A week locks at your day count and counts toward belts. Diplomas land as weeks 2, 4 and 6 end. Leave any time (a restart begins at week 1); the program picks back up moved ahead by the weeks you locked. You can run it again later.

**Reminders** sit at the top of the menu: pick a time and the days you want a nudge to train, then tap Allow notifications. Work-It has to be saved to your Home Screen for them to work. No reminder on a day you already trained or once your week is locked, and you can turn them off any time.

**Belts** are diplomas for locked weeks stacked over the year. They unlock at weeks 2, 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 42, and 48. You always see the one you are aiming for. Belts feature character artwork based on your chosen gender track (Male, Female, Non-binary), which you can change in Edit profile.

The house also has a **scoreboard** (who trained, how much iron moved) and **medals**. A coach voice (Tom, Grey, Luna, or Eli) talks to you in the app and in your personal mail (welcome, recap, badge, belt). Mail the whole house gets — nudges, the weekly scoreboard, the six-week pace check, release notes — always comes from Eli. Prize and punishment are the same six things: growth, lean, definition, power, stamina, mobility.

## First-week advice

1. Put Work-It on the **home screen** (Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen). Do not live in a browser tab.
2. On Home, tap **Start Next**. Do the day it shows. Do not shop the whole year. The work is already picked.
3. Pick weights that feel **controlled**. Leaving one or two reps in the tank is fine.
4. Log every set. The log is the training.
5. Rest Wednesday. Hitting your chosen number of days locks the week — that is enough.
