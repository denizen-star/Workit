The whole game
Start whenever you're ready and move at your own pace. Gold means do this. Hit the days you picked at join — default is 4 — and the week locks. They don't have to be in a row.
1
Start it
Tap the gold Start WO. Left one open? It says Resume WO and picks up where you stopped. Travel flips the whole day from Select Workout.
2
Move through it
Weight and reps are already filled in. Set effort 1–5 before you tap Complete Set or start a timer. Rest runs itself — skip it if you're ready. Need a different move? Alt Exercise swaps that one lift.
3
Finish it
Warmup or cooldown is extra credit — run, bike, stretch, core, yoga, or abs. Then Finish it, rate the session, and see the recap. Hit your days and the week locks.
Later the week shifts to one lower, Extra Upper, and a bonus core or class. Help covers that when you get there.
